01 — ABOUT ME

From Geography to Geospatial Problem Solving

I am a First-Class Geography graduate from Kaduna State University with a growing specialization in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing and spatial analysis.

My interest in geospatial science grew from observing how rapidly urban development changes the landscapes around us. During my undergraduate research, I used satellite imagery, GIS and land-use/land-cover analysis to investigate the encroachment of urban development onto agricultural land in Kaduna North.

I am continuing to develop my skills through practical GIS projects, remote sensing courses, data analysis, mapping and geospatial technologies.

My long-term goal is to apply geospatial technology to environmental, urban, agricultural and humanitarian challenges while making complex spatial information easier to understand and use.
